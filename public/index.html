Distance is measured in miles.

Time zones that never line up. Goodnight texts instead of goodnight kisses. Two cities, one calendar full of someday.

02 — the truth

But relationships are measured in moments.

Nobody remembers the hours spent waiting. Everyone remembers the night you stargazed on a call, the game you never finished, the letter that unlocked at midnight.

So we built a place for those moments.

Not another video call. A little shared world that's quietly yours — with a sofa, a window, a record player, and a cat. When one of you moves, the other sees it. Live.
